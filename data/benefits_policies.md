# Meridian Health Group - Employee Benefits & HR Policy Handbook (Synthetic)

> Internal reference for the People Operations team. This is a SYNTHETIC document
> created for training. All plan names, numbers, and vendors are fictional.
>
> **Handbook edition: current as of 1 October 2025.** Treat this date as "today"
> when answering employee questions about what is in effect now, what changes
> next year, or what applies on a given date. Do not use the real-world date.

## POL-001 Benefits Eligibility
Full-time employees (30+ scheduled hours/week) are eligible for all health and
welfare benefits on the first of the month following their hire date. Part-time
employees scheduled 20-29 hours/week are eligible for dental, vision, and the
retirement plan only. Contractors and interns are not eligible.

## POL-002 Open Enrollment
Annual open enrollment runs the first two weeks of November, effective January 1.
Outside open enrollment, changes require a qualifying life event (marriage,
birth/adoption, loss of other coverage) and must be submitted within 30 days.

## POL-003 Medical Plan Tiers
Three medical tiers are offered:
- Bronze HDHP: lowest premium, $3,000 individual deductible, HSA-eligible.
- Silver PPO: mid premium, $1,500 individual deductible, broad network.
- Gold PPO: highest premium, $500 individual deductible, lowest copays.
Coverage tiers: Employee Only, Employee+Spouse, Employee+Children, Family.

## POL-004 Dental & Vision
Dental covers 100% preventive, 80% basic, 50% major, with a $2,000 annual max.
Orthodontia has a separate $1,500 lifetime max. Vision covers one exam per year
and a $150 frame allowance every 24 months.

## POL-005 Health Savings Account (HSA)
Available only with the Bronze HDHP. Employer seeds $750 (individual) / $1,500
(family) per year. 2025 IRS contribution limits apply. Funds roll over and are
portable if you leave.

## POL-006 Flexible Spending Account (FSA)
Healthcare FSA allows up to $3,200/year pre-tax. Dependent Care FSA allows up to
$5,000/year. Healthcare FSA funds are use-it-or-lose-it with a $640 carryover
into the next plan year; the carryover applies to the Healthcare FSA only. The
Dependent Care FSA has no carryover and no grace period - unused funds are
forfeited at plan year end. You cannot enroll in a Healthcare FSA and an HSA in
the same year.

## POL-007 401(k) Retirement & Match
Employees may contribute pre-tax or Roth. Employer matches 100% of the first 3%
and 50% of the next 2%, for a maximum 4% match. Match vests over three years
(one-third per year). Auto-enrollment at 4% applies after 60 days.

## POL-008 Paid Time Off (PTO)
Accrual is tenure-based: 0-2 yrs = 15 days/yr, 3-5 yrs = 20 days/yr, 6+ yrs =
25 days/yr. Maximum carryover is 5 days into the next calendar year. PTO accrues
each pay period and is available as it accrues.

## POL-009 Sick & Safe Leave
Separate from PTO: 6 paid sick days per year, front-loaded January 1. Unused sick
days do not roll over and are not paid out at termination.

## POL-010 Parental Leave
12 weeks of paid parental leave at 100% pay for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks
for the secondary caregiver, available for birth, adoption, or foster placement,
within the first 12 months of the event. Runs concurrently with FMLA.

## POL-011 Remote Work Stipend
Fully remote and hybrid employees receive a one-time $500 home-office setup
stipend and $50/month toward internet. Reimbursed through the expense system with
a receipt. Not available to on-site-only staff.

## POL-012 Life & Disability
Company-paid basic life insurance equals 1x annual salary. Short-term disability
pays 60% of salary for up to 12 weeks after a 7-day waiting period. Long-term
disability pays 60% after 90 days.

## POL-013 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
All employees and household members get 8 free confidential counseling sessions
per issue per year, plus legal and financial consultations. No enrollment needed.

## POL-014 Tuition & Learning Reimbursement
Up to $5,250/year for accredited coursework or approved certifications with a
grade of B or better. Requires manager pre-approval and a one-year stay agreement.

## POL-015 401(k) Employer Match - 2026 Update
Effective January 1, 2026, the employer match changes to 100% of the first 4%
of eligible pay (up from 3%), and vesting becomes immediate upon the match
being credited (up from the three-year graded schedule). This policy supersedes
the match percentage and vesting terms described in POL-007 as of the effective
date; the auto-enrollment default and the pre-tax/Roth contribution choice in
POL-007 are unchanged and continue to apply. Before January 1, 2026, POL-007's
match and vesting terms remain in effect.

## POL-016 Sabbatical Leave
Employees become eligible for an unpaid four-week sabbatical after five years
of continuous full-time service, available once every seven years, intended for
personal or professional development.

Sabbatical requests are reviewed by People Operations and approved only for
employees who have completed at least seven years of continuous full-time
service, subject to manager approval and business needs at the time of the
request.

## POL-017 Bereavement Leave
Up to 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child,
parent, or sibling). Up to 2 paid days for extended family (grandparent,
parent-in-law, or sibling-in-law). Requests are submitted to People Operations
within 30 days of the event; no documentation is required for the first request
in a calendar year.

## POL-018 Jury Duty & Civic Leave
Employees called for jury duty or a subpoenaed court appearance receive paid
leave for the duration of the service; the company pays the difference between
the court's stipend and the employee's normal pay - it does not pay full salary
in addition to the stipend. Separately, employees receive up to 2 paid hours of
voting leave if the polls are not open for at least 3 consecutive hours before
or after their scheduled shift.

## POL-019 Relocation Assistance
Employees relocating more than 50 miles for a company-initiated transfer receive
a lump-sum relocation assistance payment of up to $7,500, which is taxable
income. If the employee voluntarily terminates employment within 12 months of
the relocation, the assistance must be repaid on a pro-rated basis: one twelfth
of the total is forgiven for each completed month of service after the
relocation date.
